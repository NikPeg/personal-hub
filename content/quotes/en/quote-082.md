The more you believe you can fit everything in, the more you take on and the less you ask yourself whether each new commitment is truly worth a portion of your time. Your days fill inevitably with things that don't interest you much. The faster you move, the more you resent tasks that resist acceleration; the more frantically you plan for the future, the more anxiety any uncertainty provokes. And the more control you gain over your time, the lonelier you become.
