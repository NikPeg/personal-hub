I knew that life was supposed to be more joyful, fuller, more meaningful — that the world is actually more beautiful than what we see. We shouldn't hate Mondays and live for weekends and vacations. We shouldn't have to raise our hands to ask permission. We shouldn't be kept inside four walls one sunny day after another.
