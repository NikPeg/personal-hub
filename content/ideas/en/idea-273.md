# Projectors in the corners of the room. You can broadcast anything you want onto the wa

Projectors in the corners of the room. You can broadcast anything you want onto the walls: firstly, change the wallpaper pattern at least once a week. Secondly, you can hang widgets - weather for today, CLOCK, exchange rates. Thirdly, you can watch movies, of course.
We need to figure out how to intuitively manage all this. It’s clear that it’s from a phone, but how? Using AR? Like point the camera, place it there
You can make it turn off when the lights are turned off when a person goes to sleep
You can do it on a schedule, or make a separate switch. Another question that arises is where to hang it all. Like if the broadcast is on the opposite wall, then when you approach it you are already blocking it
What if you hang it directly above the wall? Like a protrusion of a few centimeters, and somehow manage to broadcast
When you switch to movie viewing mode, you can also edit it in AR: like editing a picture, you edit the screen - stretch/compress, move
I was also thinking about QR codes mb thread
To determine exactly where the wall is, so that everything is clear
By the way, widgets can be very large, for example, a to-do list for today
