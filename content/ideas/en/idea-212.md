# Bot for tg for outline around stickers

Bot for tg for outline around stickers
You send him a file with a transparent background with a sticker, he automatically makes an outline (simple along the contour with rounded edges)
You can separately adjust the thickness of the outline
Ideally, he would immediately add a sticker to the set, but, as you remember, bots in tg cannot communicate
