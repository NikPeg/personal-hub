# A bot that collects information on all banks, etc., taking into account mat. expectati

A bot that collects information on all banks, etc., taking into account mat. expectations shows how best to allocate the budget for a certain period of time

UPD: if you stupidly calculate the mat. waiting for each option and adding up, then the winning option is to simply buy the security with the highest checkmate. waiting for all the money. Intuitively it seems that this is not the best option (when one company goes bankrupt, you lose all your money)

UPD: probably the task needs to be formulated more precisely. Add to the Decision a list of requirements of the form: probability of loss 50% ≤ 1%, probability of loss 90% ≤ 10%, etc.
