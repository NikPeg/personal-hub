# New art - dancing in smoke. Multi-colored smoke (steam), the movements of the dancer c

New art - dancing in smoke. Multi-colored smoke (steam), the movements of the dancer control its flows. You can put gloves on your hands, as in swimming - so that a wave of your hand moves a whole stream of smoke. You can use a large number of clothes: skirts, like gypsies, dresses, like in Ancient Greece
Probably 3 types:
1) heavy gas of different colors is supplied from above, the dancer seems to be hiding from it
2) light gas is supplied from below
3) under pressure, gas with a density ~equal to the density of air is supplied by fans from the side
