# Duels of workers. The point is

Duels of workers. The point is
Any person can apply for another person's place. To do this, he needs to come and do the same work as someone called to a duel. If he does significantly more/better quality in a day, then the employer can hire him instead of the first one
This will solve the problem of a lot of people working in the wrong jobs. This way they will at least realize that a person working there is better than him
