Triangle who the hell knows

1. Most startups die because there is no need for their product. This immediately gives rise to the idea of ​​using Maslow’s pyramid of needs as a guide when searching for needs.

2. But, if you look at the truly popular services, you get the impression that they are not aligned according to Maslow’s pyramid. And they are spread out, as if on a wind rose, in a triangle, at the vertices of which there are only three basic needs - “to be lazy more”, “to have more fun” and “to earn more”.

3. The ideal startup promises all three things in one bottle. Excellent - two. Good - at least one. But if we want to force them to work hard to get a well-deserved reward, this, as a rule, does not work.

4. And if we organize our startup along this triangle, what do we promise our users? Nothing? Hmmm
