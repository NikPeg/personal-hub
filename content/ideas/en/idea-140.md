# There are screens everywhere that shine with polarized light. All people wear glasses 

There are screens everywhere that shine with polarized light. All people wear glasses that have different degrees of polarization, that is, they transmit only one light. Screens sense a person's approach (for example, using Tag'go) and show personalized content. Cheap and in-demand technology
