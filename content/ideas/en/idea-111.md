In fact, you even completely turn a blind eye to the fact that learning ability greatly depends on the time that a person allocates to learning. Like, how can we know which cards to show him if we don't know how long he's going to spend learning them? You can, of course, say that it is better to learn 50% normally than 100% somehow, but...
MB that's why QL is made like this. Like there you see all the cards, gradually learning EVERYTHING little by little. What do you say?
Solutions:
1) for each hashtag, indicate how long it will take to train (prediction based on a neural network)
2) Ask the person how much he is going to spend on it?...

Well, no, the task is clear: to allow a person to learn as much as possible. “learned” means he swiped the card to the right, and nothing else. If, according to the neural network, he will “learn” the most in this way, then so be it. Less - what has already been learned will simply be forgotten
