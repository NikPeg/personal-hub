# Calls in English

Calls in English
For convenient communication in English
Imagine going home and opening the app.
Call, a random interlocutor is there, you communicate
Then beeps, there is a new interlocutor
You can add friends, see which of them are online, and call them later

In my opinion, this idea was simple for communication, now it’s also for learning

Another idea is to add conversation topics so that people know what to talk about
