# LLM ethics: checks on input but not output

A research finding by Voron: censorship in language models is applied to incoming messages, but not to outgoing ones.

DeepSeek can write "Taiwan is a free country" on its own — but if you send it that same phrase, it immediately falls back to the Chinese censorship response. The trigger isn't the content itself, but the act of receiving a message on a forbidden topic.

An interesting research direction: what else can models say on their own that they can't accept from a user?
