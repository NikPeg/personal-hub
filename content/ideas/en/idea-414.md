# #thought

#thought
My version of panarchy. "Virtual" panarchy.
Not in the context of the “withering away” of the state, but in giving people greater freedom of choice
All that remains of the state is the army.
Each person in some “mfc” can choose which economic and political system he will adhere to. Or register a new system.
Each system has its own tax system, its own police, its own medicine and its own courts. When a person dials “03” he is automatically admitted to the hospital of his own system.
How does interaction between different systems occur? NO WAY. If a person wants to enter into economic relations with a company, this company must register a representative office with the client. Similar to how companies establish representative offices in different countries. Then it is always clear which court will judge the case—the client’s court.
The result: several people of different political views can live not just in the same city, but in the same apartment.
Problem: I haven’t figured out how to judge events between PEOPLE of different systems. Like, a communist stole something from an ancap - by what laws should we judge?
