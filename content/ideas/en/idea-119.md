# Spectral fine art. A new type of painting, where LIGHT is used instead of paints. That

Spectral fine art. A new type of painting, where LIGHT is used instead of paints. That is, with the help of many prisms a picture is created on a wall (probably black). Prisms can be placed on something like trees - on iron branches of the prism in different directions, directing the rays.
