# Disposable heating pads

Disposable heating pads
The problem with modern heating pads is that it is not clear where to put it when the water cools. They are just starting to freeze. Throw out the water?
The solution is kiosks or vending machines near the metro that sell disposable cellophane heating pads. When the heating pad cools down, the person simply throws it away
In my opinion, a brilliant solution to the cold problem. The batteries are too small for this

UPD: A more mundane, but better idea. Sell in all five stores sets of ~100 pieces of plastic bags in the shape of heating pads. People fill their homes with hot water and go to work warm. When it cools down, throw it away.
But the idea of a small shop selling them has not been canceled. Continuation - a jacket with hot water inside it
