# Profession - idea generator

Profession - idea generator
A person spends 4 hours a day reading information about new startups in order to stay up to date with the modern world - who is successful and who is failing. The remaining 4 hours a person walks, listens to music, does what he wants, most importantly - alone with his thoughts. The goal is to generate several ideas per day.
And use such people as workers, that is, pay them a fixed salary WITHOUT RISK.
Next, the ideas pass into the hands of sociologists, they compose surveys, interview people about the need for such a product
The necessary ideas pass into the hands of architects and are implemented
