# Pet food preparation kit. After all, in fact, there is nothing unusual in their food, 

Pet food preparation kit. After all, in fact, there is nothing unusual in their food, and it is not clear why EVERYONE feeds them store-bought food, although they cook for themselves. It would be possible to make a gradual transformation of society:
1) expensive kit for preparing food for cats (Instagram store)
2) When it reaches the peak of popularity, create a portal where you can post a variety of recipes for cats. You can also order a supply of rare ingredients (which are difficult to find in the store)
3) When this method of earning reaches its maximum, start concluding contracts with pet stores for the supply of those rare ingredients (MB with recipes on the packaging)
4) Limit - contracts with grocery stores for the supply of ingredients
(5) Create a factory yourself to produce these components for animal food
Possible problems:
1) people won’t want to cook food for animals in their dishes (lol of course, same meat). So much the better - you can sell a special “pan” for cat food
2) animals need to cook very little, perhaps some will think that it is easier to buy a pack than to cook so much. To solve this problem, it is necessary to indicate everywhere that the food received can be stored as purchased.
