# Control your mood with music

Control your mood with music
For computational linguistics, it is almost no longer a problem to understand a person’s mood from the text he writes. We set up neural networks to analyze messages that the user writes (this is already done), and...
We select music that suits his mood. You can even continue the idea: a person sets the ideal of his mood in some settings:
1) motivated to work
2) mysterious
3) depressed
...
Then the algorithms create a sequence of songs that will take him out of his current state and lead him to the one he needs, which he indicated! Then a person will have a way to control his feelings. Maybe not 100%, but what an achievement this would be for humanity!
