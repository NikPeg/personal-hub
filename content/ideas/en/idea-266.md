# Cosplay cafe

Cosplay cafe
A cafe that changes its style every month according to major fandoms.
The interior, the clothes of the waiters, the appearance of the room, the menu, and drinks change.
Examples: BAR from Treasure Island. Inside the barrel, there are bones on them. Behind the bar is a dude dressed as John Silver. Fresh ROM served.

Bar "Inside Lapenko". All the waiters are made up to look like Lapenko, even the women with the mustache. There are Soviet TVs everywhere, playing Channel 9, and there are carpets on the walls and floors. They serve oleveshka, herring under a fur coat...

Bar for mathematicians - a million jokes. The bartender only pours two glasses of beer, you cannot order one. Classic menu - Pies, Fibonacci chips for 123.58 rubles, etc.

You can make it a month of cyberpunk. Robot fights, waiters to be painted cool...
