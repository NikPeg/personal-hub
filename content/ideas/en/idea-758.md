From the end of 2025, it became noticeable: the market began to overestimate companies that make money on subscriptions and licenses. Investors have seriously accepted that SaaS subscriptions are no longer a perpetual annuity. And in January 2026, this feeling intensified: a new wave of sales began, and it became clear that this was not about an accident, but about a shift in the logic of how software is created and purchased.

To put it quite simply: before, you paid because “doing your own” was expensive, time-consuming and difficult. Now a new reality is emerging: making your own has become quick and cheap. Not everywhere and not for all tasks, but already enough for the “vendor lock” to begin to dissolve. And this is not philosophy - this is practice.
The most obvious example I hear now is about China. Teams of vibe coders and outsourcers appear there, who assemble software for entrepreneurs personally for their processes: CRM, automation, internal offices. They do it literally in a couple of days and for modest money. Against this background, local SaaS companies begin to panic: contracts are not renewed, discounts are becoming more and more aggressive, but this no longer helps, because it is easier for businesses to go fast, adaptive, and on their own.
