# Fake people

Fake people
Particularity, authenticity brings people together. For example, knuckles in VRChat: they lasted so long precisely because of their meme nature. If they didn’t have this authenticity, they would play and stop.
It's the same with nations. The peoples of the Caucasus, Bulgaria, Ossetians - all have their own culture and traditions. Thanks to them, they remained in infinity. Moreover, they make a lot of money from souvenirs.
Idea: create a fictional people. With history, tradition, origin. All with a smart look, but the more stubborn, the better. Share as much as possible on social networks. Make a place of attraction for him - at least a stall where they sell souvenirs. Maintain a wiki history where everyone can contribute what they see fit. And make money from souvenirs)
