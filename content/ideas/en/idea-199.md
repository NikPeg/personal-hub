# Company for writing beautiful texts

Company for writing beautiful texts
Website/bot
You write the text simply randomly
Without thinking what it looks like
You send it there, and in about 20 minutes they rewrite it for you to make it beautiful
Can be used for both messages and work texts
You pay per word
How to implement - split between statements by, for example, paragraph
They rewrite, then one person reads the entire text so that there is no lexical repetition
And you're done
In the future - a neural network
