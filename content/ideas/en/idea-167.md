# Futures on distant stars

Futures on distant stars
America withdrew from the space treaty with the USSR. Now I know how they can boost their economy: sell plots on the Moon. Without having access to them! Then the sale of futures for mineral extraction on the moon will begin, shares of lunar companies for the construction of hotels there... And all this before the flight to the Moon!
You can write a story about this. For example, two superpowers are starting a war to redistribute the universe. However, none of these countries have yet entered space! It’s like dividing the skin of an unkilled bear:
—The USSR claims its right to the planet Mars.
—The USA refuses.
*USSR drops nuclear bombs on a peaceful city*
—The USSR once again declares its right to the planet Mars.
—The USA recognizes this right for the USSR.
