# Kit for creating quests at home

Kit for creating quests at home
This box, like all sets, can be sold on the website, on social networks, or in children's stores
Put all sorts of sensors there, and the instructions describe a bunch of ways to create different quests with their help. Then you can sell personalized sets - for a birthday, pirate quest, spy, New Year, etc.
