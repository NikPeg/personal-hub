# The story "The Kumeri Brothers"

The story "The Kumeri Brothers"
70s. Two twin brothers. Both are maniacs. Even in their youth, with the help of threats and murders, they “removed” one of them from all documents, and began to live one life - everyone thought that this was one person. Thanks to this, each of their murders had a perfect alibi: during the commission of the murder, the second one was actually seen by many people.
But of course, one of them could find himself in a situation that the other did not know about, which could arouse suspicion. Therefore, once a week they met in a secret place, where they told each other in detail the events of the week, and also exchanged thoughts. This is the most important thing

They had to create ONE personality. So that none of his acquaintances would suspect that yesterday he was a communist, and today he is a capitalist. Therefore, during these meetings they had numerous highly intellectual disputes, as a result of which both took the same position on issues of politics, culture, art

They did not commit murders very often. But then one day an unlikely event happened: both killed at the same time. It turns out that neither of them had an alibi. The next time they meet, they lash out at each other, both blaming the other. Well, nothing, we sat down and began to think about what to do.

They are smart people, which is what many years of “unity” of two brains has led to. They calculate the steps of the investigation in advance, process all options so that suspicion does not fall on them, and plant evidence on other candidates for murder. But weeks after weeks go by, and even the investigator has not come to see them. The “investigation” goes on only in their heads. Naturally this starts to make me nervous

Gradually their conversations drop. They no longer discuss any art, they go over the evidence against themselves again and again and all that. It takes up their whole life. In conversations they increasingly shout at each other, leading to fights

In short, the main part of the story is the moral decline. Ordinary people would be tormented by their conscience, but in this case it’s just an illness. As a result, after another fight, one of them says that he will inform on the other. The first responds in kind

A two-prisoners dilemma in its purest form begins. They don’t have any meetings after this, the plots run in parallel. Everyone understands that if his brother denounces him, he will be imprisoned, but his brother will remain free, no matter what he says. And everyone understands that it is more profitable to convey it first
