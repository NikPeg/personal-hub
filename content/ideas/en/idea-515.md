# A work reflecting modern reality: with memes, VKontakte correspondence, etc.

A work reflecting modern reality: with memes, VKontakte correspondence, etc.

I would like to study the market thoroughly, create something that people really need

Perhaps I want to make a new art format: "messages". Like my diary, for example. But from different people. So that people can easily import their messages into this human-readable format

You can check the applicability of the format on fan fiction. For example, write a fanfic about Ladybug and Super Cat using this format. MVP: channel in tg

Two opinion polls: for writers and for readers. Do writers have this problem? Do readers have such a need?

Ideally, an application for quickly “exporting” life into text of a similar format. That is, take a whole chat and a couple of conversations, indicate the start and end time, and turn them into easy-to-read “text” from messages.
