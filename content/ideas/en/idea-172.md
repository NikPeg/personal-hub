# Website whattoread.rf

Website whattoread.rf
Hire people (as the site becomes more and more popular)
A person comes in, writes his thoughts, what interests him now
One of the site managers reads this and selects a book/film/series/song for him to read now, something suitable. Very often people think about something that others have already thought about. Giving them the opportunity to find out what exactly will help a lot
If the manager does not know what to advise, he passes it on to the next one
Of course, recruiting people will be very difficult. We need well-read, smart people
I forgot to write - the site user pays money for all this to pay employees’ salaries
