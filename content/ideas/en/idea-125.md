# At subway crossings where advertisements used to hang, hang paintings

At subway crossings where advertisements used to hang, hang paintings
The choice goes like this. There are QR codes hanging at the bottom and top of the escalators - voting for the paintings. Once a month a painting is re-elected - if more people voted for the new one than for “keep”, it is changed. The artists themselves can submit paintings. Or some famous paintings
