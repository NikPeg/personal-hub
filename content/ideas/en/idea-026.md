# Subscription to life

Subscription to life
What is a Netflix subscription? Everyone pays the same price and gets access to an infinite number of series. The price is the same, regardless of your needs. This is due to simplicity: so that people do not think about their money. It will probably be the same with all of life in the future. A person pays one price and gets access to everything.
To car sharing - to any car on the street at any time
To any vacant apartment owned by this company (as a rental)
To any amount of food in all restaurants included in this program
If you somehow limit the sale of the company’s products outside (for example, all things purchased under the program belong to the company, and a person does not receive ownership rights over them, but only the right to use), then even:
To the products of the company's participating stores
To things (clothes, shoes)

It's starting to come true
