# SNOWFLAKE GENERATOR

SNOWFLAKE GENERATOR
Study the theory of the formation of snowflakes, write an algorithm (mb neural network) that generates them. Remember, there is not ONE pair of repeating snowflakes on earth! This means that on this site, each user can be assigned their own snowflake. Your personal snowflake! No one else has one like this!
Continuation - galleries of snowflakes, so that people can sit and look at them, choose their favorite ones
