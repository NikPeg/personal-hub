# Huge screens for the rich

Huge screens for the rich
Sell TVs the size of a country house, waterproof. Hung on the wall. You can sit at them like you would at a computer, so as not to damage your eyes, or use them as a TV or cinema. In your free time from work, you can display animation or colors of the house on it (new every day)
