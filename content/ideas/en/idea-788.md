# People-AI hybrid as a new trend

Companies used to keep a staff of cheap workers for simple tasks. Now those workers need to be paired with AI: primitive tasks like "look at what the app looks like" are done by a human, everything else by AI.

In other words, embed a human in the AI pipeline exactly at the points where AI fails.
