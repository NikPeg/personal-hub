# The language should have as many synonyms with different sounds as possible. Especiall

The language should have as many synonyms with different sounds as possible. Especially frequent words: I, you, this, prepositions. Then a person could say the same thought with different sounds, as if in different languages. So it would be possible to combine the melodiousness of Russian, the severity of German and the affectionateness of Japanese in one language
