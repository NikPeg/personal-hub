# Shop without price tags

Shop without price tags
In a world where people do not spend much time looking for low-priced goods, in a world where the price of food has become insignificant compared to the intellectual sphere of life, there is no place for price tags. Why see how much food costs if you won't buy it anyway, no matter the price? This only develops greed and the pain of overpaying every day. A person either trusts the store, which means he will go into it, or he doesn’t trust it. He trusts those he trusts that the price is exactly what it should be. Thus, the idea is a store for the rich without price tags
