# Information about the place that is relevant to a person. We once thought about "field

Information about the place that is relevant to a person. We once thought about "fields of knowledge". That is, a person enters a store, and all the information about this store immediately appears in his memory, so he will immediately know where everything is. That is, publicly available information, like Wikipedia or stackoverflow, but tied to a place.
So now I thought that this is not very cool. If everyone is given all the information, you will not understand it. You need to highlight the main thing. But how? And it’s very simple: neural networks. That is, a reader who approaches “Glina-17” will be recommended jokes about shit, and an art connoisseur will receive useful information about it.
