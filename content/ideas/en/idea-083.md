# A device for transmitting taste.

A device for transmitting taste.
I just saw a dude on the bus, he’s sitting chewing on his earphone. So, another wire branches off from the earphone and goes into the mouth. Using electrochemistry, it produces:
Sweet taste
Salty
Bitter
Acidic (oxidizes)
The MB also changes the temperature. This way people can, for example, scroll through photos of food on Instagram and feel their taste.
