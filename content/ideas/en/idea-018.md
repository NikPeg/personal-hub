# A new type of cinema is a dream. Films for ~20 minutes. The dream is characterized by:

A new type of cinema is a dream. Films for ~20 minutes. The dream is characterized by: an illogical narrative, blurry, indistinct images, bright colors. The goal is to put the viewer into a dream-like state. Complete shutdown of logic and internal thoughts. For example, in a dream, a character can turn into another before our eyes
