# Use the rest of intellectual workers

Use the rest of intellectual workers
A large number of professions - janitors, cleaners, gardeners - do not require mental stress. Anyone can do them. In every mental profession there is a time of rest - when a person gets tired of working and takes a break. I propose to schedule this time and give mental workers another role - physical. For example, a cleaning programmer gets up once an hour and washes the floor in the right place. He receives a higher salary than he could have received without working as a cleaner. This is beneficial for both the worker and the employer (no need to hire additional staff)
