# TagGo (copied from paper in a folder)

TagGo (copied from paper in a folder)
(other names: Tag'n'go, Taggo, Tago)

The goal is to unite the real and virtual worlds. A smartphone is a person’s eyes and ears, so they need to be connected to the environment around him.

The form is probably an application. But also the material part - tags, markings. Any device that distributes wifi or bluetooth. Moreover, the smartphone does not need to connect to them: this all takes a long time. The smartphone “sees” the tag and punches its mac-address into the Tag'go database. Now the virtual object, the smartphone, has a connection with the real world. There are many further options. The user may receive a notification that redirects him to the desired site. You can leave it in the application: add remote control functionality to the application for controlling air conditioners and other household appliances.

Most likely, the “page” of the item is something like a Google questionnaire. A way to directly and quickly interact with physical objects.

Implement “favorite” tags that you always need access to. Implement the ability to “complain” so that the tag can be blocked in the application (to avoid advertising).

Partial list of applications:
Automatic elevator floor selection
Recognition by the phone of the room in which the user is located - a notification with a transition to the desired application/website (for example, recipes or news in the kitchen)
Controlling household appliances from your phone (air conditioner remote control)
Opening the car instead of a key (like in a Tesla)
Road accident notification (the person involved in the accident can send a “message” to all Taggo users nearby)
Notification of stations/stops in public transport
Connection to screens in subway cars for additional. information
Check in without ancient cards at work/school
Open the app to count calories when approaching the refrigerator
Creating convenient surveys/tests
Open the menu/food order panel in a cafe
Offer an interactive map, list of promotions and discounts in any store
Ability to conveniently donate to musicians on the subway
Buying a ticket at the cinema
Obtaining information about an exhibit in a museum
The ability to “rate” any establishment

On Wikipedia about Bluetooth 4.0 it is written that the Bluetooth module can live for a year on one coin cell battery. Therefore, the idea of ​​sticker modules is quite realistic.
