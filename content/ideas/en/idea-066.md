# WikiAnswers website. A place where people all over the world write answers to popular 

WikiAnswers website. A place where people all over the world write answers to popular questions, including editing other people’s answers. The thing is that on Wikipedia the information is very general - for example, the article about Santa Claus is HUGE. If a person needs to find out where Santa Claus lives, a separate page with all the possible answers to this question would be enough. Maybe something like: first, abbreviated answers, links below them - detailed information on this issue
