# Skyboarding

Skyboarding
Entertainment for the rich is already a full-fledged business. Large quadcopter. A man is tied to him on a banjo. They tie it by the straps so that it is in an upright position. In your hands are joysticks like on any quadcopter (one is forward, backward, turn right, turn left, the other is down, up, left, right). There is a parachute on the back. On your feet - a snowboard, or something lighter and larger. Because of the up-and-down movements of the banjo and because of the control of the air currents on the board, a person can fly and make various figures. Possible on and in the clouds
