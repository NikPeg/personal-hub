# Ideal language

Ideal language
Take the alphabet of all the sounds of humanity (I saw an article on Wikipedia somewhere - all sounds from all languages are collected), take words according to frequency of use, assign them sets of sounds according to length (excluding unpronounceable ones). Create a language in which information can be conveyed in the shortest possible way
