# Application for profitable purchases

Application for profitable purchases
Application/site/bot
The user selects the stores where he wants to shop (those near his home)
And enters a list of products that he needs (such as meat, fish)
When the user comes to the store, the application shows him a shopping list - what is the cheapest in this particular store
You need to take into account the shelf life of the products - a product with a shelf life of a month should be purchased at the best price per month
You need to add a feature - the user can remove a product of this type (something too cheap, for example) if he doesn’t like it, and it will no longer appear in the shopping list
