# A computer game based on the award-winning cartoon based on the work of Dali. Dalí wro

A computer game based on the award-winning cartoon based on the work of Dali. Dalí wrote that he wanted a visit to his museum to leave people feeling like they were in a surreal dream. It's the same with the game. Lack of logic, a person will need to show maximum non-standard thinking in order to complete the game. Random should play a big role. Somewhat reminiscent of Monument Valley, but with greater beauty and illogicality
