# Be your own HR: a personal IT contacts database

Build and maintain a personal database of people in IT — who works where, at which company, in what role.

Why it matters:
— Get referrals into target companies through warm connections instead of cold applications
— Find people for your own projects without recruiters
— Understand the market from the inside: who's moving where, which teams are interesting

Essentially: become your own recruiter. Don't wait to be found — actively maintain a graph of useful connections and entry points into companies (in Notion, a spreadsheet, or wherever).
