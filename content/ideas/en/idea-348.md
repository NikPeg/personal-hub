#thought
Psychological experiment.
We take a large sample of people. We put them all in different situations and see their reactions.
As a result, we will create a database about the likelihood of a person’s action depending on his psychotype. For example, 800 out of 1000 people similar to this one did this in this situation. This means that the probability that this person will do the same is 80%. With such a database, it is possible to lay the basis for predicting human behavior and, in the future, predict any action with great accuracy.
