# Website: "Will the AI bubble burst?"

An indicator site where an LLM aggregates daily news and estimates the probability of the AI bubble collapsing.

Something like the Doomsday Clock — but counting down to the end of the AI era. The needle moves based on real signals: investments, layoffs, product failures, regulatory risks, hype metrics.
