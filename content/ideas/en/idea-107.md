Memorization

The question arose of what to do with unknown cards. Asking randomly until a person answers correctly is not an option. After all, nothing will remain in the memory at all (it will be forgotten in a second). So far I’ve come up with the following, although it sounds rather strange: ask for the same card until the person answers “true”. Then send it to 2, 4, 8... That is, so that the person really memorizes it
The second option is to take about five unlearned cards (as I do) and ask them one by one until they learn them. Don't know
