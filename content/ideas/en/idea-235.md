# Application of a protective field for mass purposes. Protection of people and pets. It

Application of a protective field for mass purposes. Protection of people and pets. It is worth thinking about the following: weak touches should not be blocked (for example, stroking), but strong ones should. That is, the protective field must determine the force (moment?) of an approaching object and turn on/not turn on. This can probably be done using gravitational waves. They diverge in front of the object, from them (by redshift) you can determine its mass and speed, and therefore momentum
