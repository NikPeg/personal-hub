# Strategy games for programmers

Strategy games for programmers
Most games like cards or board games (dominoes) are endlessly boring, because when playing for a long time they come to the point that a person finds a strategy and is guided by it. It becomes boring and tedious
You can make a website, or just play it yourself, where each player writes a strategy for a game, and the strategies of two players are played against each other in many games. Then you can accurately and quickly understand who wins
Formally: a site where the main games are listed:
domino
card games
popular board games
And a convenient API for working with them is provided. Programmers on vacation write their strategy and fight with each other. You can make game ratings, general battles with everyone - to spark interest in the game
