Son, hello! I was driving here, thinking, some thoughts for your piggy bank. A WB store requires building a constant purchasing and advertising process. Otherwise, sales are cyclical, sluggish or falling. This requires retroanalysis and forecasting of consumer demand. We see a system whose input is the purchase history of a specific WB store, the history of advertising campaigns carried out and the history of views of store products with a time reference (to assess seasonality cycles and connections with holiday dates). As a result, the system provides recommendations or even a plan for advertising and purchasing activities. Under the hood is something based on optimization algorithms and AI. Correlating sales with time periods or specific dates catches the interest of buyers before holidays or, for example, before the summer or vacation seasons. The correlation of purchases and views with advertising shows whether advertising works or not. Next, optimization links these things and creates schedules of specific activities that improve store performance. WB can build such functionality into its marketplace as a regular paid service.
