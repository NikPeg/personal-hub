# A book about a man whose life corresponded to the books he reads. Moral dilemma: what 

A book about a man whose life corresponded to the books he reads. Moral dilemma: what is it: a coincidence, an omen of fate, or the weakness of his spirit and submission to the authors?
It is advisable to leave an open ending so that the reader himself thinks about whether his life is being dictated by books
By choosing the books he reads, he himself insisted on his life
Stages of awareness:
1. Try to choose good books
2. Give up books and live your own life. GG becomes empty. Noticed emptiness in my thoughts
3. Create as much as you consume. Write as many books as you read. In the end it turns out that this is one of the main character's books
