# Website freework.com

Website freework.com
Its task is to use the free labor force of millions of schoolchildren, students, and beginners.
Now many of them have a problem - what to do?
You need to write term papers, draw logos, do projects
On this site it would be possible to gather employers who do not care about order deadlines and these newfags
Like the employer thinks, it would be cool to make an app for my business on Android. But it won’t bring much money, you can live without it
He puts it on freework
And go ahead - all sorts of students do
You can organize competitions, like who can do it better
You can hold competitions like hackathons
This gives beginners the following motivation - their product will be useful, unlike what they make themselves
And dudes who would like to bring them to life, but are not ready to spend money on it, can post their ideas there
