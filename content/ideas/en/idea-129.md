# Levels of abstraction in philosophy. Most definitions fit any level of consideration. 

Levels of abstraction in philosophy. Most definitions fit any level of consideration. For example, the degree of freedom can be considered both from the point of view of all atoms in the universe, and from the point of view of 1 person and his things. One could do something like "at any level of abstraction"
