# Formalization of philosophy. Create a developed portal about philosophy (perhaps super

Formalization of philosophy. Create a developed portal about philosophy (perhaps supervised by some institute), where strict answers to all philosophical questions would be collected. The most general approach. That is
The science of physics, for example, was structured like this: a theory is expressed, an experiment is found to refute it, a new theory is expressed.
Mathematics is like this: a theory is expressed, an error is found in it, a new theory is expressed
The same can be done with philosophy. For example, Plato said this and that, 500 years later Descartes objected to this. Thus, a person, having read the development of philosophy on each issue, will be able to find the answer to any question
Here it is worth considering the following: Not all people have the same axioms, that is, attitudes towards different things. That is, a person for whom love is more important than duty may come to opposite views than someone who has the opposite. That's why
1) Before entering the site, let the person prioritize each of the basic feelings (or what is more basic?)
2) When a person moves from one statement of a philosopher to another, give him a “branch”, a conditional operator - what is more important to you?

This idea is actually very important. A person can make mistakes for many years without understanding simple things. Having read the right book, he could live his life completely differently! The purpose of this site is to collect the achievements of all mankind on every question of human knowledge.
