# The human brain is weak

The human brain is weak
The achievement of humanity is that it has grown as a society
Different people took on different tasks
Is it the same with LLM?

Make the VILLAGE shorter
Everyone will have the task of collecting as many donations as possible
It will be their job
They can communicate with each other, reproduce, etc. (how do different people reproduce?)

How about having parents write a prompt for their child?
The upbringing process is interesting yes

How about making males and females?

Similar projects
Yes, there are several analogues! Here are the main ones:

1. Generative Agents (Stanford, 2023)
25 AI agents live in the virtual town of Smallville
Communicate, plan, remember
Throw parties, fall in love
Similarities with your idea: social interaction, memory, autonomy
2. AI Town (a16z, open-source)
Open implementation of the concept above
Agents walk, communicate, develop relationships
Difference: no economy/donations, no evolution
3.AutoGPT/BabyAGI
Agents with goals, break down tasks
General: autonomy, goal setting
Difference: no society, one agent

MVP 1: One agent + donations
Hypothesis: People will pay to communicate with a personalized AI character

One bot with character
Streams/posts content
Accepts donations
Metric: $ per agent per month
Analogs for studying: Character.AI, Replika

MVP 2: Multiple agents + social
Hypothesis: Agent interaction is more interesting than solo performance

3-5 bots communicate with each other
Spectators watch + donate to favorites
Metrics: engagement, watch time
Analogues: AI Town, Twitch Plays Pokemon (collective interest)

MVP 3: Competition for resources
Hypothesis: Scarcity creates drama → more engagement

Donations = "lives" of agents
Who does not reach the threshold → is eliminated
Survival mechanics
Metrics: donation growth, viewer return
Analogues: The Hunger Games, Battle Royale formats

MVP 4: Reproduction and Evolution
Hypothesis: Viewers invest in “dynasties” of agents

Successful ones create "descendants" (new prompt = mix of parents)
Spectators cheer for the lines
Metrics: retention, emotional connection
Analogues: Neopets, Tamagotchi, genetic algorithms
