# Create professional organizations. Goal: Many companies don't need a WHOLE person. For

Create professional organizations. Goal: Many companies don't need a WHOLE person. For example, they don’t need a full-time manager; 2 hours a day would be enough. Moreover, I am sure that in ALL companies the work that a worker of a certain profession must do is not a multiple of the amount of work that one worker can do. So they have to hire more than they need and overpay
It would be possible to put this work on stream. For example, an accountant who works in 5 companies at once. The task comes to him in general terms; he doesn’t even have to know which company he works for. Like in sports programming
Computers can handle the distribution of labor. MB even receives an order from the company, the bot forwards it to one of the hundreds of workers of this company. Almost like freelancing
The problem is with determining the quality of work.
(Okay, stop! The problem with freelancing is that a person needs to get involved for a long time, to understand what it’s about. That’s not what this idea is about. Just let a person work in several companies at once, so that he doesn’t get overpaid, and he works around the clock)
Formally: a company where workers come. Companies come and choose the workers they need. They take exactly as many HOURS a day as they need - no more. This is how we get a worker who works the entire working day with maximum employment.
