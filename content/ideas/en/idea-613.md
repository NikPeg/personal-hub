# something like a leisure agency

something like a leisure agency
I see a problem with a lot of my friends: they earn enough money, they have relationships, but they cannot provide stable leisure time for themselves. That is, they suffer because there are few events, they are too lazy to organize, etc.

You could first become yourself, and then create an agency with the following scheme: a person is added to the gazebo of friends or their interests are described in detail, they tell where they went, who liked it, who didn’t. They give approximate addresses, who lives where.

The task of a leisure agent is to monitor events in the same area where everyone lives. Periodically add information about them into the conversation, create polls about who will go, etc. If enough people have gathered, organize everything, book, buy tickets. Mb even call a taxi

Target audience: wealthy youth. MVP: website/VK group/tg channel + launch of advertising + free ~month of work if a client is found. Monetization: monthly payment + interest on organizing events
