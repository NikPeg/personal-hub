# Entertainment industry for cats

Entertainment industry for cats
1) tablets with scratch-resistant screens to show cats games and videos
2) attraction. Monitors the cat's behavior and its reaction to what is happening on the screen. In accordance with this, the picture changes. For example, a mouse runs away. Using cameras/rangefinders/infravisors
3) development of intellectual abilities of cats. Educational games. Find out why cats don't perceive other cats on video. If we understand, we can show TV series/films
4) use food as a stimulus for the cat. Type for good results - food
