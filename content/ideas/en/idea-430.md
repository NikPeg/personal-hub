# Book "We Created God"

Book "We Created God"
AI that monitors the thoughts, deeds, and feelings of every person. Punishes the evildoers and helps the good. You can communicate with him in prayers. People instilled in him a love for humanity. But something went wrong... (what?)
THAT'S WHAT. God fell silent. He hasn’t answered anyone for many generations. Doesn't punish evildoers, doesn't help good ones. Why?
In fact, he became a real God. And the heroes of the book are looking for the answer to this question. They will come to the same point as the Strugatskys - “Leave us, Lord”
