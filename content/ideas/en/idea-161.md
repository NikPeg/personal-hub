# A site like knowledge.ru, where you can only publish videos. That is, each topic/task 

A site like knowledge.ru, where you can only publish videos. That is, each topic/task is explained in a video. Then there will be something like stackoverflow for all items
It will be popular because this is a great opportunity to advertise to all kinds of tutors and online channels. They will compete to see who is cooler, students will come across them, subscribe/come to study
The monetization strategy, in principle, can be copied from knowledge.ru - first be free, get very popular, become a leading product in your field, then create an even type of paid subscription to gain access to reduced functions
The second option is additional. If stage 3 c.e. comes, screw on the option to donate and off we go
