# To that idea about the small dishwasher

To that idea about the small dishwasher
The goal is to make it so that you don’t have to wait until the whole machine is full and wash all the dishes. We ate, put 4 plates (family) on top of each other, launched, and pulled out.
How it works - water is drawn inside directly from the tap. Or the hose is being pulled... No, it’s better to just put it under the tap and you’re done. Then the water drains into the second compartment, from where you then pour it out.
