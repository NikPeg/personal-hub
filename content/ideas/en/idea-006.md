# "Give me a loan" website

"Give me a loan" website
Each user describes why he needs money and why he doesn’t have it. Each one has a page with history and photographs. Rating of how many times he lent, how much he borrowed, how much he returned. People come in and think about whether to lend him money or not. Like microloans based only on trust. From good people to kind people.
