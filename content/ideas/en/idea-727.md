# how to make a cool article

how to make a cool article
example
gil comes out at the end of 2025
We take a model trained on the Internet, early 2025
we ask him what assumptions he sees about how gil “will” work
He makes a few guesses

We ask the new model to write experiments that will test these assumptions
Let's see which experiment doesn't meet expectations
We are publishing an article about him

That is, gil in this case is such a “black box”
Its unpredictability is interesting
What works as predicted is not interesting
