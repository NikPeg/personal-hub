# Theater with robots

Theater with robots
The person will have a constant feeling of the uncanny valley. This will allow you to show famous works from a new side.
For example, "Frankenstein, or the Modern Prometheus." The main role is a man. All other robots. Here a person will feel everything the other way around, the one who should cause horror is normal, the rest are not. Same with Flowers for Algeron. The main character is a man. The rest are robots. This symbolizes the fact that he, developmentally retarded, is the only one left human when everyone else is not.
