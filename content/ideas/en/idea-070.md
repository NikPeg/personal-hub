# Groups for reading school literature and old posts by Durant (and other groups)

Groups for reading school literature and old posts by Durant (and other groups)
Follows from the previous idea. So that a person can read something old, while being part of a team. Reading memes and jokes in the comments.
You can start at the beginning of the school year, when people still have a lot of time and ambitions.
You can create a general group like “Reading!”
And it contains links to groups of specific works:
"Reading! The Master and Margarita"
"Reading! War and Peace"
Etc. Start with the school curriculum in mind. In the description, indicate how long it is published and when we finish reading
Separately study how many books a person can read at the same time
ChiTimes mb

How to deal with spoilers: make the bot delete a comment if “spoiler” is written under it. There are bad sides, but how else?

How to create a main group: either a site menu with all the books, or posts that a new group has appeared

In the main group you publish polls like “what other books do you want to read?” “How many books do you read in ReadTimes”, etc. Also the type “556,776 people are already reading the book “prom” with us!”

Run advertising in “literature memes”, groups about school, or in chat groups
