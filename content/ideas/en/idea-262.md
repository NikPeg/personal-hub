# For a fantasy story

For a fantasy story
Why do people not like public transport? Because they have to be not where they want. Where do they want it? Mostly at home. Therefore the idea. A new type of transport - a room!
Combine with the idea about tilting the cars so as not to feel the turns. Hoba, a huge box on wheels underground. Huge multi-story rail system. In the mos.ru application you set a schedule for where you want to be at what time (the application writes the minimum time when possible). You wake up, open the door... And you're at work! A replacement for teleport is a movable house.

Problems: what about families who work in different places? Maybe several rooms are connected into an apartment, but after some time they have to be disconnected. Well, everyone's going to have to be alone for a while

Perspective: work is also mobile. Perpetual motion! What then is the meaning of the concept of “time”?
