Bring the farm application, of which there are a bunch, into real life

It will also be watered by a person - at first it will also be watered by a worker, then you can automate it (microcontrollers will also come in handy)
Naturally, each plant is monitored 24/7 by a camera, you can monitor its growth
A person can, there, buy himself additional space, even more
