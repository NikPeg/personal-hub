# Childhood Friends and Character

Friends from childhood. Influence on the subconscious. Raised by them. Love from childhood.

Now in plain language. Dmitry Sergeyevich Likhachev wrote in *Letters About the Good and the Beautiful* that childhood is a period of closeness, so lifelong friends are acquired in deep childhood. But he did not explain why. I have a hypothesis.

Freud wrote that a person’s character is mostly formed by childhood. Impressions received in childhood leave a lifelong mark and make a person who they are. When two people become close in childhood, they strongly influence each other and “raise” each other. One person’s character adapts to the unevenness of the other’s character, and vice versa. That is why even as adults they feel easier together: their characters fit.

This suggests a thought: the best wives might be the people children have known since childhood. No irritating traits and so on. After all, it is quite easy for you with O.

Yet for some reason children do not socialize much in childhood with children of the opposite sex. Most likely because of jokes from parents, teachers, and classmates.

Now I may understand the policy in India where the family chooses whom the son will marry: his childhood friend. That may be why many such marriages are happy.
