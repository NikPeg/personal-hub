# Helping from Above and Below

There are two ways to help people: from below and from above. From below is clear: help the poor, the hungry, the sick. From above is less obvious: help the rich by creating astonishing inventions that improve their lives.

The unexpected part is that after several decades, those inventions reach the poor and become mass products, as happened with phones.
