# Comments and Trust

There was also the thought that after reading anything, it is right to write a comment. There should be some result of what you have read, so you do not become a dull consumer of information.

How to earn people’s trust:

1. Find something in common when you meet: same hometown, same name, a mutual acquaintance.
2. Mirror the person’s style of speech and writing. Joke if they joke; use parentheses and emojis if they do.
3. Find shared interests and discuss them. You can also adopt something from them; the person will feel useful.
