# Meaningless and small

Claude told me yesterday that I need to learn to find things I enjoy on my own.
But I'm just a bundle of thoughts and genes... Meaningless and tiny compared to an infinite universe, compared to infinite time.

What actually has value for me? History? Ideas?

Loving a person is just as meaningless — that person is just as small.

Love itself? The roles we perform? Art?

Friendship? Projects? Novelty?

It seems like what matters to me is that a project is mine. For my own thing I'm willing to learn marketing, sales, anything. For a work project — I don't care.
