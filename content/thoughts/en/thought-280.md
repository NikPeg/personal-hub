# Searching for external motivation

I remember when I took out a mortgage on an apartment
I thought it would motivate me to earn more money
And I do love money
But I ended up just not caring and barely even think about it
Isn't that exactly searching for external motivation?

Right now I'm also thinking about finding a girlfriend, leveling up my career, or developing aesthetically
What's the point of all that? Isn't it better to do what I actually need...

But this logic breaks against urgency. With every year the number of options shrinks in almost every direction of growth. Fewer jobs, fewer available girls...

Maybe try to frame it like a pyramid
Not trying to master each sphere perfectly
But growing to the next level in each one...
Distributing tasks across levels
Ugh, complicated
