# Lessons About Love

What conclusions do we draw from this story?

1. In love, you must not lie. Under any circumstances. It is too bad, and it will only make things worse for you later.
2. Date only someone you are ready to marry and live with for the rest of your life.
3. If you no longer feel love, break up immediately. Otherwise you risk losing the one.
4. And the main thing: any shyness is punished.
