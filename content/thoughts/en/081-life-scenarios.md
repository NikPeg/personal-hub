# Inventing Scenarios for Life

Invent scenarios for life: for something interesting to happen, you first have to invent it.

For example, tell a guy we are conducting an experiment: how long can a person sit staring at a wall, without any phones? Then somehow casually tell his girlfriend that he was seen with another girl. She gets jealous and asks: “Where were you last night?” He says: “You won’t believe it, but I was staring at a wall.”
