# Beyond Plain Text

I scold myself for having one-dimensional thinking: I write everything down in cards and plain-text files. I think about making diagrams and schemes—two-dimensional forms. But why only 2D? Sherlock Holmes remembered everything in a three-dimensional “memory palace.” Maybe 4D or 5D is possible too.
