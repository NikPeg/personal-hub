# The Brand Growth Curve

Brands develop along an S-shaped curve: slow growth at first, then fast growth, then slow growth again. First they are unknown, then they occupy their market.

For the user, the rising part of the curve is especially useful: the company already makes quality products but has not yet earned broad popularity, so it sells cheaply. How can this observation be used? One could create one company of a given type each year, but later ones would already use the influence of earlier ones, so selling cheaply would make less sense.
