# Rewarding Study Without Rewarding Cheating

Children should be encouraged in their studies. But paying for grades means encouraging cheating and negotiation skills, and those will not help on the real exam.

So the proposal is this: once a year or once every six months, arrange a mock exam, using problems from material already covered, and pay a fixed amount. For example: 50 thousand for a perfect score, 40 thousand for an A, 30 thousand for a B — something like that.

Naturally, the child should be watched for all four hours, perhaps even with cameras.

In principle, one could also pay for victories in recognized olympiads, possibly even more. But there is again a chance of cheating, although what exactly can one cheat from on olympiad problems?

For example:
- OGE/EGE with an A — 30 thousand
- OGE/EGE with a perfect score — 35 thousand
- level 3 olympiad — 40 thousand
- level 2 olympiad — 45 thousand
- level 1 olympiad — 50 thousand
