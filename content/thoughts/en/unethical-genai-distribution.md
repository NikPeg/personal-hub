# The Spread of Unethical GenAI

Perhaps “unethical” GenAI will soon spread the way drugs used to spread: models capable of creating a virus or explaining how to cook meth.

But who would be willing to fund that? Maybe gray-market businesses like casinos?

And there it is already: FraudGPT, DarkBert, and XXXGPT.
