# Real Feelings and Games

I realized that books and films do not make me feel things fully. In real life, feelings are a million times stronger: they seem to strike through the whole body.

Maybe in books and films a person is watching someone else's life, while in reality they are living through their own problems, so the immersion is deeper. That suggests that feelings in computer games can be stronger, because the player identifies with the character and fears dying as that character. Even losing an in-game item can feel tragic. Maybe that is one reason games are so popular.
