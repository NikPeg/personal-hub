# Grooves Left by Solved Problems

“Every solved problem leaves a groove in your brain along which thought will pass while proving a theorem” — Cauchy, though I do not remember the exact quotation.

This corresponds exactly to the structure of the brain. After motivation, neuron weights increase in reverse order. That is the very groove.
