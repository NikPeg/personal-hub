# Business as an Experiment

Start every business as an experiment.

Theory: there is a significant number of people for whom reading books together is more interesting than reading separately.

Experiment: create a group where a new chapter is posted every day. Determine after one year how many people find it interesting.

Experimental results: the number of subscribers after one year.
