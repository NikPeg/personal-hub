# The Illusion of Stability

The greatest misconception in my life was the feeling of stability. It seemed to me that if everything was good at the moment, it would stay that way forever. But no. Life — and especially people — is too changeable for everything to remain constant without spending life energy.

You need to think always, at every moment: plan ahead, watch trends, consider possible future scenarios — even the worst ones. And before every action, except rest, ask yourself: what is its purpose?
