# Pattern Reincarnation

The last three works I read are connected by a shared idea: something great, some superbeing, finds patterns similar to itself in the past or future and relocates into them. This includes the protagonist’s reincarnation in iPhuck 10, a hero in one author’s work, and the Kids in Hyperion.
