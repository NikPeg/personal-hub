# The Evolution of Cultures

Toward thoughts about the evolution of cultures. Cultures do not die by themselves. They are displaced by stronger ones. When the strongest appears, the others either adapt or die. For example, a people ready to work twelve hours a day will force other peoples to work the same amount.

The Greeks would not have wanted to build up their army if not for the Spartans.

One could try to invent the currently ideal culture. People live underground in bunkers: there is no point in building missile defense against them — shoot as much as you want, you will not reach them. Culture and entertainment are banned: only study and work. For reproduction, take an example from a fictional system: 100% birth control.

The question of public dissatisfaction arises. Total control? For now, let us not think about the transition to this order. The task is only to formulate it.

Another example: China increasing GDP while neglecting spending on entertainment. Others will have to do the same or lose.

And the method of transition is very simple: raising children as in a fictional society. Different religions for different personality types? A camera embedded in the forehead and a GPS tag.

Addition after reading The Selfish Gene: one must account for the fact that not all cultures are ESS. That is, when dissatisfaction with life becomes too high, culture obviously changes.
