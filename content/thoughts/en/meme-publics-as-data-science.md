# Meme Publics as Data Science

If you treat the world of VK public pages as a data-science industry, it resembles laboratories in large companies. A lab has a very small chance of making its own discovery, but it is needed in order to quickly reproduce another company’s achievement. That is why scientists read papers and so on. Ideally, meme publics should work the same way: your task should not be to create your own meme, because the probability that it will take off is very small. The task should be to watch competitor publics, quickly find their meme sources, and post faster than they do. Meme creation is another question. You could train a predictive model for which memes subscribers will like and which they will not, then, knowing their tastes, start generating content: predicting what will appear in meme publics before the meme itself appears. What can be triggers and sources for memes? The most obvious answer is news. When a major news story appears, half a day later every public posts memes about it, and it seems those memes are not that hard to predict.
