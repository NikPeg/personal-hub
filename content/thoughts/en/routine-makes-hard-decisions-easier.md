# A Flow of Difficult Decisions

Ender’s Game, war games—the thought that people find it easier to make difficult decisions when those decisions are put on a conveyor belt.

Roughly: if a person has to turn a key and launch a missile once in a lifetime, it is hard. But if drills happen every week, and the person does not know for sure whether it is a drill or a real situation, they will not be as nervous.

How can this be applied in life?
