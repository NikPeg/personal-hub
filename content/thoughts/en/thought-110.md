# The Sprint-Month Principle

The sprint-month principle: a new project every month, not IT. For example, a meme group, news, art, an online store, a music band—anything. You do it for a month, then at the end of the month look at the growth rate. If it is unsatisfactory, you drop it. This way you will find something truly needed.
