# Persuasion Through Trusted Sources

To convince a person of something, first of all you need to find out whom they trust, and then build on their sources.

An acquaintance once gave an example of how he convinced a religious person by using quotations from the Bible as arguments, even though he himself does not believe in God and had not read the Bible.
