# Virtual Panarchy

My version of panarchy is “virtual” panarchy. Not in the sense of the state “withering away,” but in giving people greater freedom of choice.

Only the army remains from the state. Every person, in some kind of public services center, can choose which economic and political system they will follow, or register a new system.

Each system has its own taxation, police, healthcare, and courts. When a person dials “03,” they are automatically sent to the hospital of their own system.

How do different systems interact? They do not. If a person wants to enter into economic relations with some company, that company must register a representative office in the client’s system. Similar to how companies open representative offices in different countries. Then it is always clear which court will hear the case: the client’s court.

Result: several people with different political views can live not only in the same city, but even in the same apartment.

Problem: I have not figured out how to judge events between people from different systems. For example, a communist steals something from an ancap—which laws should apply?

Authoritarian leaders are valued more in companies.
