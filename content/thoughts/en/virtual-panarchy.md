# Virtual Panarchy

My version of panarchy is “virtual” panarchy: not in the sense of the state “withering away,” but as a way to give people more freedom of choice. Only the army remains from the state. Each person, through some kind of public-service center, can choose which economic and political system they will follow, or register a new one. Each system has its own taxation, police, medicine, and courts. When a person dials the emergency number, they are automatically routed to the hospital of their system. How do different systems interact with one another? They do not. If a person wants to enter economic relations with a company, that company must register a branch in the client’s system, just as companies open representative offices in different countries. Then it is always clear which court will handle the case: the client’s court. Result: several people with different political views can live not only in the same city, but in the same apartment. Problem: I have not figured out how to judge events between people from different systems. For example, if a communist steals something from an ancap, which laws apply? Authoritarian leaders are more valued in companies.
