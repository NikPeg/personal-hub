# Meme Publics as a Data Science Industry

If we view the world of VK publics as a data science industry, it resembles laboratories in large companies. A lab has a very small chance of making its own discovery, but it is needed to quickly reproduce another company’s achievement. For that, scientists read papers and so on.

Ideally, meme publics should work the same way: your task should not be to create your own meme. The probability that it takes off is very low. The task should be to monitor competitor publics, quickly find their meme sources, and post them faster.

Meme creation is a separate question. You can train a predictive model: which meme will work for subscribers and which will not. Knowing their tastes, you can start generating content — that is, predicting what will appear in meme publics before the meme has even appeared.

What can be meme triggers or sources? The most obvious one is news. When a major news story appears, half a day later every public posts memes about it, and it seems not too difficult to predict them.
