# Flexible Private Views and Public Expectations

Moral foundations and political views should not get in the way of earning money. One should do what is profitable. And one does not necessarily need to tell others about it.

The economy determines your life. It is an upper limit that prevents you from taking more from society than you give. Therefore, inner views should be flexible, while external views should be the ones people expect to hear from you.
