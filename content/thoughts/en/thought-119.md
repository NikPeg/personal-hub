# A Database of Human Reactions

A psychological experiment. Take a large sample of people, place all of them in different situations, and observe their reactions. In the end, we would create a database of the probability of a person’s action depending on their psychological type. For example, 800 out of 1000 people similar to this person acted this way in this situation. That means there is an 80% probability that this person will do the same. With such a database, it would be possible to lay the foundation for predicting human behavior and, in the long term, predict any action with high accuracy.
