# Helping From Above and Below

There are two ways to help people: “from above” and “from below.” From below is clear: help the poor, the hungry, the sick. From above is less obvious: help the rich instead. That means creating astonishing inventions that improve their lives. The unexpected part is that after several decades these inventions will reach the poor and become mass-market. Phones, for example.
