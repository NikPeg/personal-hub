# How to Choose What to Read

“Read this...”
“No.”
“Don’t criticize it until you’ve read it!”

Choosing what to read is a serious and important process. The world has too much written, said, and filmed for anyone to see it all. In one lifetime, you need to manage to encounter what matters most. So not everything people recommend is worth reading.

By what criteria should we choose? Everyone decides for themselves. There are several factors before the book is even in your hands:

1. Fame. It matters in a way: a good book becomes known over time.
2. Recognition. Classics and the like should come first.
3. Title. No, this is not “judging by the cover.” The title is part of the book, and an important one, because people judge by it.
4. Description. If you are deciding whether to read something, read the description. Spoilers...
