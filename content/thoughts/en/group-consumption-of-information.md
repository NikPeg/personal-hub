# Group Consumption of Information

A human being is social, so people prefer to consume information in a group, surrounded by others interested in the same thing. For example, watching a show: a new series creates a kind of social subculture. Remember *Game of Thrones*? New episodes could be discussed and commented on. This is also where the popularity of social networks comes from: comments and reposts.
