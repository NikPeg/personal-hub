# A Cryosleep Colony

In the age of space conquest, if cryosleep exists, colonization might work like this: a colony is sent to a nearby planet, and almost everyone is frozen. At first, only a few people live on the ship and build new homes with all conveniences. Then they thaw more people, who start living there and building the next homes. In this way, everyone is thawed exponentially.

Before being frozen, you could order what kind of house should be built for you.
