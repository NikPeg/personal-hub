# Cryosleep and a Colony

In the era of space conquest, if cryosleep exists, maybe it will work like this: a colony is sent to a neighboring planet, and everyone is frozen. At first only a few people live in the ship and build new houses with all amenities. Then they thaw people, who start living there and building the next houses. And so on, exponentially, until everyone is thawed.

Before freezing, you could order what kind of house should be built for you.
