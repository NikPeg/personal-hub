# The Market and Predictable Fluctuations

Remember you said that on a universal exchange the price of flour would rise in winter and fall in summer? Remember dad said bonds rise before payment?

That is wrong. This is exactly the invisible hand of the market. Suppose it was true for a while. Then smart people would start buying and selling, winning on that jump again and again. Their turnover would grow until the jump balanced out. So in an ideal market there are no fluctuations at all. There are only unpredictable ones.
