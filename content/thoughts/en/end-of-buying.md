# The End of Buying

I generally think the concept of “buying” will eventually outlive itself. Something like serfdom may happen, when there was no land except landlords’ land. The same could happen with everything: one day all apartments in Moscow will be bought by landlords and rented out. Something similar may happen with car sharing too.
