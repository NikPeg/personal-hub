# Inventing Life Scenarios

Life needs scenarios. For something interesting to happen, it first has to be invented.

For example, tell someone we are running an experiment: how long can he sit without a phone, staring at a wall? Then casually let his girlfriend hear that he was seen somewhere suspicious. She asks where he was last night, and he says, “You will not believe it, I was staring at a wall.”
