# Religion, Tact, and Upbringing

The most important thing in a person is religion. The second most important is tact. Only after that come intelligence, knowledge, money, and everything else.

This should be conveyed to children in their upbringing. The striving for goodness and tact are formed in childhood. That is the most important thing.
