# The Lazy Worker Method

You can treat yourself like a lazy worker on your own team: how do you get that person, meaning yourself, to work as productively as possible?

For a while I lived by a schedule of one hour of work, half an hour of study, and half an hour of rest. It worked for some time, then got stale: I started getting distracted and zoning out. So I began setting tasks for the day. In the morning I write a list, and when everything is done, I rest. As a result, I want to finish faster so I can be free. We will see how it works.
