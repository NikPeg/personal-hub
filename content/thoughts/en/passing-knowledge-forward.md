# Passing Knowledge Forward

I want to carry forward, to future generations, different ideas and values that I share. A person chooses what to pass on.

Remember in Fahrenheit 451, people memorized books because they were being burned. Each person chose a book that was especially valuable to them. It is the same with humanity.

If you do not carry the knowledge you consider important to the next generations, someone else will. But will they manage it?

What if the knowledge is forgotten, as with Demidov's method? Values are one thing, but this is about knowledge and skills.

For example, P. passed the knowledge of l3manager to me. Now I am developing it according to his guidance.
