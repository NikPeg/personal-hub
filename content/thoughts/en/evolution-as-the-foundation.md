# Evolution as the Foundation

Now, understanding the evolutionary nature of culture, we can apply natural approaches to culture. For example, cooperation between social media communities and bloggers is an example of kin selection. Mutually beneficial altruism evolutionarily wins over selfishness. Amazing: I see the foundation of everything — evolution.
