# The Great Being Reincarnation Pattern

The last three works I read are connected by a shared idea: something great, some super-being, finds patterns similar to itself in the past or future and moves into them. This is the protagonist’s rebirth in *iPhuck 10*, Max in Max Frei, and Kid in *Hyperion*.
