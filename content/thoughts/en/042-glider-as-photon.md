# A Glider as a Photon

So I do not accidentally forget.

Remember that in Conway’s Game of Life there is this glider?

```text
  0
    0
000
```

It turns back into itself and flies at a 45° angle. This is very similar to a light wave. There, too, electrical oscillations turn into magnetic ones, and the wave moves. So the triangle is a quantum of light in Life.
