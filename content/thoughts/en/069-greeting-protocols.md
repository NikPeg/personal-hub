# Greeting Protocols

Two modems exchange codes. A guy and a girl want sex. “Good afternoon! Sincerely, ...” Two tribes perform a half-hour greeting dance with changes of carnival costumes.

Can you stop this masquerade, walk up to the chief of another tribe, pat him on the shoulder, and say: “Hey, come on, I’m just like you!”?

You cannot. Because if he walks up to you, you will think: “Oh God, he is plotting something.” So we became modems transmitting greeting protocols.
