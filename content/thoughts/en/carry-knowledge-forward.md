# Carrying Knowledge Forward

I want to carry forward different ideas and values I share, passing them on to future generations.

A person chooses what to pass on. In Fahrenheit 451, people memorized books because the books were being burned, and each person chose the book that was especially valuable to them.

It is the same with humanity. If you do not carry the knowledge you consider important to the next generations, someone else will. But will they handle it well?

And what if the knowledge is forgotten, like some rare method? Values are one thing, but this is about knowledge and skills.

One mentor passed on knowledge of a work tool to me. Now I am developing it according to his guidance.
