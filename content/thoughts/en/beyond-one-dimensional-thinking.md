# Beyond One-Dimensional Thinking

I criticize myself for one-dimensional thinking: I write everything in cards or plain-text files. I think about diagrams and little schemes — two-dimensional ones. But why only 2D? Sherlock Holmes remembered everything in a three-dimensional “memory palace.” Maybe 4D or 5D is possible too...
