# The Harm of Fiction

Perhaps fiction is completely useless, even harmful, because it gives people the wrong idea of life. Authors write what they would like to see in life: ideal women and so on, closing their eyes to reality. After reading a book, a person expects the same from life, but that is unrealistic.
