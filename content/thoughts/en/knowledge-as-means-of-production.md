# Knowledge as a Means of Production

Knowledge as a means of production.

Part of the brain can be an asset that brings profit or happiness. There is also the opposite side: parts of the brain, habits, and connections that get in the way.

For example, when you learn a new skill, connections form between neurons that can later bring you income.

This knowledge needs to be maintained, just like a machine tool or a car.
