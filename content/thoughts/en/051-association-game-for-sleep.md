# Associations for Falling Asleep

I’ll write this down so it does not get lost, though I understood it quite a while ago.

To fall asleep, I play associations with myself: you think of a word and immediately move to whatever it is associated with. Crimea — shore — Zhivchik — bear — car — I.

As a result, thoughts become not logical and structured, but tangled. Because of that, the brain falls asleep very quickly — it feels like about five minutes.
