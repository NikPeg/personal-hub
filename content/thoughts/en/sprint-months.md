# The Sprint-Month Principle

The sprint-month principle: every month, start a new project, not necessarily in IT. For example, a meme group, news, art, an online store, a music group—anything. Work on it for a month, then look at the growth rate. If it is unsatisfactory, drop it. That way you can find something genuinely needed.
