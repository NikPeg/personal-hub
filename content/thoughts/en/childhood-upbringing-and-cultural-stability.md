# Childhood Upbringing and Cultural Stability

The foundation of cultural stability is uniformity in the upbringing of children. In childhood, the subconscious is formed, and it determines receptivity to the memes of a culture. Change the method of upbringing, and you will get an entire generation of people who “do not like” their culture.
