# Fiction as Problems to Solve

Fiction is like a set of problems. The brain has to understand on its own what the author meant. Nonfiction or journalism is the answer to the problem: the reader does not have to strain their mind to understand. Another aspect is abstraction. In journalism everything is concrete and clear, leaving thought little room to unfold. A work of fiction opens many possibilities for thought in the mind.
