# The Researcher Mode for People

Sometimes you want to understand a person. Something unknown and incomprehensible in another soul gnaws at you. A researcher mode turns on: you want to learn and learn.

And then suddenly it is over. You realize that you understood. You realize there was nothing especially deep there, and you searched in vain.
