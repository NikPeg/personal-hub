# Army vs. Science

Army versus science.

I was thinking about why the army structure—complete obedience to orders and a strict hierarchy—does not spread into other areas of life, for example IT companies. I concluded that in the modern world it is not as important to make a product quickly as it is to make it well. Therefore a subordinate can say to a manager, “I think you are mistaken here and here.” The manager will consider it and make a better decision.

Still, this idea can be used when something truly needs to be done quickly. For example, at hackathons: agree that the team lead’s orders are not discussed. Time is very limited—two days.
