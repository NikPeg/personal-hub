# Army and Science

Army vs. science.

I was thinking about why the army structure — complete obedience to orders, strict hierarchy — does not spread to other areas of life, such as IT companies. I concluded that in the modern world it is not as important to make a product quickly as it is to make it well. Therefore, for example, a subordinate can tell a boss: “I think you are mistaken here and here.” The boss will consider it and make a better decision.

However, this idea can be used when something truly needs to be done quickly. For example, at hackathons: agree that the team lead’s orders are not discussed. Time is very limited — two days.
