# The Digital Age Elitizes Art

The digital age makes art more elitist.

Before the internet, only major creators could survive through art: writers whose books appealed to the mass reader, and painters with mass demand. Now, with the development of the third stage of the digital economy, a mass artist may lose their turnover. Each person will find the artist suited specifically to them. An artist from a group with a million subscribers may turn into a group with twenty-plus subscribers who donate enough for them to live.
