# Using Human Laziness

In reality, people are incredibly lazy. Not lazy in the sense of saving time, but lazy in the sense that they do not want to think. For example, in one study project I always distributed the tasks and left the largest share to some meek person so I could do less myself. In another project, by contrast, I was too lazy to listen, agreed to everything, and ended up doing the most myself. One must know how to use this.
