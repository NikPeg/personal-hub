# The End of Buying

I think the concept of “buying” will eventually outlive itself. Something like serfdom may happen, when there was no land except the landowners’ land. The same could happen with everything: someday all apartments in Moscow may be bought by landlords and rented out. Something similar might happen with car sharing too.
