# Can Evil Be Punished?

Is punishing evil the primary task? What if, before doing good, one should punish evil? Is it possible to punish evil?

How do I know that my worldview is the only correct one? Do I have the right to force other people to follow my principles?

“A doctor must not stay silent when a person is ill; likewise, you cannot stay silent when evil lives in a person.” Staying silent—no. But may I eradicate it by violent methods?
