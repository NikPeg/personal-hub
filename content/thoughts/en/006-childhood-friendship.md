# Childhood Friends

Childhood friends. Influence on the subconscious. Being raised by one another. Love from childhood.

Now, in plain language. Dmitry Sergeyevich Likhachev wrote in *Letters About the Good and the Beautiful* that childhood is a period of closeness, and therefore lifelong friends are made in deep childhood. But he did not explain why. I have a hypothesis.

Freud wrote that a person’s character is shaped mostly by childhood. Impressions received in childhood leave a mark for life and make a person who they are. So when two people come together in childhood, they strongly influence one another; they “raise” each other. One person’s character adapts to the unevenness of the other’s character, and vice versa. That is why, even as adults, they find it easier to be together: their characters fit.

This leads to a thought: the best partners would be people children had known since childhood. No irritating traits and so on. It is very easy for you with Lena, after all.

For some reason, though, children rarely socialize with children of the opposite sex in childhood. Most likely because of jokes from parents, teachers, and classmates.

Perhaps now I understand the Indian practice in which the family chooses whom a son will marry: his childhood friend. That may be why marriages are happy in many cases.
