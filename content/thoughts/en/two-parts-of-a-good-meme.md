# The Two Parts of a Good Meme

A good meme has two parts: visual and textual. The visual image should be strange and evoke emotion on its own. Such images often become backgrounds for popular memes. The textual part is closer to linguistics. A memologist’s task is to predict which word, fixed expression, or pun will become popular soon. What are such text memes based on? Probably not new Western words—the languages are too different. Do they appear on 2ch? An interesting question.
