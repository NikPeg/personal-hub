# The Two Parts of a Good Meme

A good meme consists of two parts: visual and textual. The visual image should be “strange,” producing emotion on its own. Images like that, for example, are posted by beobanka. They often become the “backgrounds” for popular memes.

The textual part is close to linguistics. The memologist’s task is to predict which word, set phrase, or pun will become popular soon.

What are such text memes based on? Probably not on new Western words: the languages are too different. Do they emerge on Dvach? An interesting question.
