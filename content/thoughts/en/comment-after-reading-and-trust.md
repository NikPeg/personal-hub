# Commenting After Reading and Building Trust

There was also a thought that after reading anything, it is right to write a comment. That gives some result to what was read and helps you avoid becoming a dumb consumer of information.

How to gain people’s trust:

1. Find something in common when meeting: from Kirov, same name, a mutual acquaintance.
2. Repeat their speech and writing style: joke if they joke; use parentheses and smileys if they do.
3. Find common interests and discuss them. You can also adopt something from the person; they will feel useful.
