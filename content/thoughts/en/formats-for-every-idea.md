# Formats for Every Idea

All my ideas and thoughts should eventually be turned into one of these formats:

- startup;
- pet project;
- research;
- article;
- Habr post;
- VC.ru post;
- Telegram channel post;
- TikTok or Reel;
- meme;
- publicistic essay;
- book;
- song;
- poem;
- talk;
- Avito service.

In general, I am thinking about my image and digital footprint online.

It would be interesting to start a pet project not just by “taking it and doing it,” but with market research. If similar things already exist, record a video or write an article about them.
