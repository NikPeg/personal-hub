# Real Feelings and Games

I realized that neither books nor films make me truly feel. In real life, feelings are a million times stronger: they seem to strike through you from head to toe. I had that yesterday, and it was amazing.

My hypothesis: in books and films, a person follows another life, as if watching someone else. In reality, you are living through your own problems, so the immersion is deeper.

From this I concluded that feelings in computer games can be stronger. A person identifies with the character and fears dying as that character. So losing an in-game item can become a tragedy. Maybe this is one reason computer games are so popular?
