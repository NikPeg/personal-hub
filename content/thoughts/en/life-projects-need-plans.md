# Life Projects Need Plans

It infuriates me that I fail to think of many actions. If someone had said, “Maybe do it this way?”, I would have done it. But because I do not think of it myself, it never occurs to me.

For example: I could have written to one girl that we were breaking up, and preserved another girl’s respect. I also could have offered to take her to the airport yesterday.

Damn, damn, damn. Is there any way to avoid such mistakes in the future?

We have already understood that the mind exists to fulfill desires. But now we formulated a desire, and the mind did not fulfill it. Why? Because it is stupid? Clearly not: I work at a major IT company.

The issue is something else. It is not adapted to solving life problems. Why? Computer games? No. A relative was right on my birthday: “Good luck with your projects. But what about love? That is also a project.” Exactly.

I need to sit down and think. Make a plan.
