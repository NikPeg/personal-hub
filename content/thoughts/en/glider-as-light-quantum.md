# A Glider as a Quantum of Light

So I do not accidentally forget.

Remember the glider in the program “Life”?

```
  0
    0
000
```

It turns into itself and flies at a 45° angle. This is very similar to a light wave. There, too, electric oscillations turn into magnetic ones, and the wave moves. So the triangle is a quantum of light in “Life.”
