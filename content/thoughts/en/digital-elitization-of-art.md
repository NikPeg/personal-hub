# The Digital Elitization of Art

The computer age makes art more elitist. Before the internet, mostly major creators could survive through art: writers whose books appealed to a mass audience and artists with broad reach.

As the digital economy develops, the mass creator may lose momentum. Each person will find the artist who fits them exactly. A group with a million subscribers could become a group with twenty true fans who donate enough for the artist to live.
