# The “Lazy Worker” Concept

You need to treat yourself the same way you would treat a lazy worker on your team. How do you make him — yourself — work as productively as possible?

For a while I lived in a rhythm of one hour of work, half an hour of learning, and half an hour of rest. It worked for a time, but then it got boring. I started constantly getting distracted by VK during work and just sitting there dumbly.

So what did I do? I started setting tasks for the day: in the morning I write out a list, and when everything is done, I rest. As a result, I myself want to finish everything as quickly as possible so I can be free. We’ll see how it works.
