# Fiction as Problems

Fiction is like a set of problems: the brain has to work out what the author meant. Nonfiction or commentary is more like the answer to a problem: the reader does not have to strain to understand. There is another aspect too: abstraction. In commentary everything is concrete and clear, leaving thought little room to unfold. A work of fiction opens many possible paths for the mind to think through.
