# A Person’s Context

Every person has a certain “context”: a field of thoughts, knowledge, and images that interests them at the moment—or perhaps always. For example, the thoughts of a homemaker and a biker barely overlap. Maybe it is possible to understand and maintain each person’s context, learning a few stories close to them in order to gain their trust.
