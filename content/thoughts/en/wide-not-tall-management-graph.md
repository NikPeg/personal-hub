# A Wide, Not Tall, Management Graph

If you become the owner or director of a company, demand a complete HR report on every employee: who manages whom. Build the graph and make sure it is wide, not tall. Someone close to me originally had one manager. Another manager was placed above him because he was not coping. Then that new manager invited an acquaintance and put her in charge of the original manager. In the end, one person does the work while three people manage them. This must not be allowed.
