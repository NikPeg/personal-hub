# A Flow of Difficult Decisions

Ender's Game, war games — the idea that people find it easier to make difficult decisions when those decisions become part of a routine flow.

Roughly: if you have to turn a key and launch a missile once in your life, that is hard for a person. But if drills happen every week, and the person does not know for sure whether it is a drill or a real situation, they will not be as nervous.

How can this be applied in life?
