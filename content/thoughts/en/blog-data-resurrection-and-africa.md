# A Blog, Digitization, and Access to Culture

The more information a person publishes openly in their blog, the more likely it is that they will later be digitized and resurrected.

Perhaps one way to demoralize Africa is to translate world literature and art into African languages. Otherwise the masses cannot consume it.
