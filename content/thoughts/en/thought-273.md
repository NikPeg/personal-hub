# Bans create gray zones that benefit the people living in them

Many things in this world are banned on purpose. Without social norms and prohibitions, gray and black markets wouldn't exist.

And entrepreneurs operating in those zones have a strong incentive for the ban to stay in place — the prohibition itself is the source of their profit.
