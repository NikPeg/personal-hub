# How to Choose What to Read

“Read this...”
“No.”
“Do not criticize it until you have read it!”

Choosing what to read is a serious and important process. Too much has been written, said, and filmed in the world to see everything. In one lifetime you need to manage to see what matters most, so not everything people recommend is worth reading.

By what criteria should you choose what exactly to read? Everyone chooses for themselves. There are several factors before the book is even in your hands:

1. Fame. It matters in a way: a good book becomes known after some time.
2. Recognition. Classics and so on should be read first.
3. Title. This is not “judging by the cover”: the title is part of the book, and a very important one, because people judge by it.
4. Description. If you are deciding whether to read something, read the description. Spoilers...
