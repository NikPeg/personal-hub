# Markets and Predictable Fluctuations

Remember how you said that on a universal exchange the price of flour would rise in winter and fall in summer?

Remember how dad said that bonds rise before a payout?

That is actually wrong.

This is exactly the invisible hand of the market. Suppose that was true for a while. Then smart people would start buying and selling, profiting from that jump again and again. Their turnover would grow until the jump balanced out. So in an ideal market there are no fluctuations at all—only unpredictable ones.
