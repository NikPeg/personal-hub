# The Social Consumption of Information

A human being is a social creature, so people prefer consuming information in a group, surrounded by others interested in the same thing. Take watching a series: a new show creates a kind of social subculture. Remember *Game of Thrones*? New episodes could be discussed and commented on. This is also why social networks are popular: because of comments and reposts.
