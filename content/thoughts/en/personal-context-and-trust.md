# A Person’s Context and Trust

Every person has a certain “context”: an area of thoughts, knowledge, and images that interests them at the moment — or always. For example, a housewife’s thoughts and a biker’s thoughts barely intersect. Perhaps one can understand and maintain each person’s context, learn a few stories close to them, and use that to gain their trust.
