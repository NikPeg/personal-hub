# Building Your Own World

The world is changeable. There used to be a beloved place, and it was wonderful. What is there now? Only videos of destruction.

There was a restaurant we went to every day. Now something completely different is there.

You need to build your own world, as independent from other people as possible, so that no one can destroy it.

If you have found a comfort zone, enjoy it fully.
