# Society Outgrows Cruelty

Anton said that in the time of Ivan the Terrible, the law had an article for “sitting on someone else’s horse.” To us that seems strange, even funny. In the 1980s and 1990s, teenagers beat people up; that still happens now too. I do not understand why. There is nothing interesting or funny in it. Probably, decades from now, our current behavior will also seem funny to people: swearing, yelling at one another, trolling. Society will likely adapt to one another. No legislation is even needed for that.
