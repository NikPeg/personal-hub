# Articles go stale — dashboards don't

Articles have a fundamental flaw: they age out fast. Today every signal says the AI bubble will deflate. Six months later the central bank raises rates, the balance shifts — but the article still sits there presenting itself as truth.

A better format: interactive dashboards that reflect reality as it changes. Plus news scraping — not an author's opinion frozen in time, but a live picture.
