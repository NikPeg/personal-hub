# The Illusion of Stability

The biggest misconception in my life was the feeling of stability. It seemed that if everything was fine right now, it would always stay that way. But no. Life, especially people, changes too much for anything to remain constant without spending life energy.

You have to keep thinking: plan ahead, watch trends, consider possible future scenarios, even the worst ones. And before every action, except rest, ask yourself: what is its purpose?
