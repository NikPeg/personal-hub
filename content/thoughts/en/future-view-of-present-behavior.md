# A Future View of Present Behavior

S. I. said that in the time of Ivan the Terrible there was a legal article for “sitting on someone else’s horse.” To us this seems strange, even funny. In the 1980s and 1990s teenagers beat everyone up; this still happens now too. I do not understand: why? There is nothing interesting or funny in it. Probably in a few decades our current behavior will also seem funny to people: swearing, yelling at each other, trolling. Society will surely adapt to each other. It will not even require legislation.
