# Intelligence and Morality

It frightens me that a person’s intelligence is not given in proportion to their moral qualities. Our world depends on intelligence, and an intelligent but evil person can cheat on women, leave children without child support through bribes, and so on.

Can it be otherwise? It seems the idea of absolute monarchy began like this: the king appoints whomever he wants. Then, if the king has high moral principles, all branches of power have them too. But can the people be sure the king has such principles? Unfortunately, no. As King Solomon says, all that remains is to pray for it.
