# No Fog Before Certainty

I have known this for a long time, but realized I had not written it down. In the anime *Weathering with You*, which we watched with K. at the cinema, there was a guy who was something like an expert on girls. He said: “until you are sure, no fog.”

And that is very good. Half-relationships and all kinds of hints never appealed to me, but I could not understand why. Now I know.
