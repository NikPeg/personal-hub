# Captured Territory Syndrome

From a conversation with P. about our relationship.

Captured territory syndrome: there is no point in making an effort to keep someone if they are already with you.

In our families, we were used to being yelled at when someone wanted to get a point across. Since we did not yell ourselves, it seemed like everything was okay. In reality, resentment was accumulating.
