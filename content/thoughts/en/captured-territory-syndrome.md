# Captured Territory Syndrome

From a conversation about close relationships.

Captured territory syndrome: there seems to be no point in making an effort to keep someone if they are already with you.

In families, we get used to being yelled at when someone wants to get a point across. Because we were not yelling ourselves, it seemed like everything was fine. In reality, resentment was accumulating.
