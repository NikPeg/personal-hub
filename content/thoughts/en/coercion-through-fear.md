# Coercion Through Fear

A way to force a person to say something or swear an oath: say that you put poison in their food or drink; tell a detailed story about an acquaintance who is a pharmacist; say that you can save them, but they must swear on their mother’s life. Or demand information.

Then put some kind of charcoal in a glass and give it to them to drink. If there is an investigation, the examination will show nothing. I will say it was a joke.

Yes, the person will not believe it later, but in the moment they may be frightened if the performance is convincing. One can quote: “Everything is poison and everything is medicine...”
