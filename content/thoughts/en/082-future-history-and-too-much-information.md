# Future History and Too Much Information

The history of the future will differ greatly from today’s history. Now historians dig through archives and collect information grain by grain. In the future there will be a sea of information — the entire internet. The problem for historians will be separating the important from the unimportant, not finding at least something.

Nothing will be forgotten at all.
