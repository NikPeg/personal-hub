# Can Evil Be Punished?

Is punishing evil the primary task? What if, before doing good, one should punish evil? Can evil be punished?

How do I know that my worldview is the only correct one? Do I have the right to force other people to follow my principles?

“A doctor must not stay silent when a person is ill, and likewise you cannot stay silent when evil lives in a person.” I cannot stay silent. But can I eradicate it by violent methods?
