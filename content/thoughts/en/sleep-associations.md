# Associations for Sleep

To fall asleep, I play a word-association game with myself. I think of a word and immediately move to whatever it suggests: sea — shore — drink — bear — car — someone I know.

The thoughts stop being logical and structured and become tangled instead. Because of that, the brain falls asleep very quickly, in about five minutes by feel.
