# The Brand Growth Curve

Brands develop along the following curve: first slow growth, then fast growth, then slow growth again. In other words, first they are unknown, then they occupy their whole market.

For the user, the rising part of the curve is very convenient. It means the company is doing everything well but has not yet earned great popularity, so it sells its product cheaply.

How can this observation be used? I thought about creating one company of the same type each year, but then the influence of the previous ones can be used in the next ones. In that case, selling cheaply makes no sense.
