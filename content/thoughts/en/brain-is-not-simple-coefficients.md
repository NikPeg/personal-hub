# People Are Not Simple Coefficients

I tried to explain human behavior through coefficients assigned to each feeling, but forgot that the human brain is a neural network with millions of layers, where every neuron in every layer has its own coefficient. A silly mistake.

Maybe I was trying to explain crowd behavior, something like psychohistory. But even there the scale is enormous: tens of billions of neurons.
