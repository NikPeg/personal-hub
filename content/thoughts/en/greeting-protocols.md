# Greeting Protocols

Two modems exchange codes. People want intimacy. A letter ends with a politeness formula. Two tribes spend half an hour performing a greeting dance with costume changes.

Can you stop this masquerade, walk up to the chief of another tribe, pat him on the shoulder, and say, “Come on, I am just like you”? You cannot. If he approached you that way, you would think he was plotting something. So we became modems transmitting greeting protocols.
