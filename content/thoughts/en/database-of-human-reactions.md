# A Database of Human Reactions

A psychological experiment: take a large sample of people, put them in different situations, and observe their reactions. The result would be a database of the probability of a person’s action depending on their psychological type. For example, 800 out of 1,000 people similar to this person acted a certain way in this situation, so the probability that this person will do the same is 80%. With such a database, you could lay the foundation for predicting human behavior and, eventually, predict almost any action with high accuracy.
