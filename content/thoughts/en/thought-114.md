# Finding Problems in Statistics

Finding problems in statistics. I think I already wrote down the idea of reading statistical surveys every day to find interesting things. Now the idea is to automatically detect “blank spots”: areas of sociology not yet covered by business. For example, in a productthon based on statistics: “69% of the population has ever participated in charity,” “68% would like to participate in the future,” but only 19% actually participate. That would clearly reveal a problem. Then you think about how to solve it.
