# Finding Problems in Statistics

I think I already wrote down the idea of reading statistical surveys every day to find interesting things. Now the idea is to automate the detection of “blank spots,” areas in sociology that business has not yet addressed. For example, statistics might say: “69% of the population has participated in charity at least once,” “68% would like to participate in the future,” while only 19% actually participate. That clearly reveals a problem. Then the next step is to think through how to solve it.
